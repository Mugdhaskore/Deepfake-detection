About this portal

Multi-layer detection for truth, trust, and digital safety

Our mission is to combat the spread of synthetic media by giving users a transparent, research-inspired platform for verifying videos with confidence.

Built around multimodal deepfake detection principles, the portal combines spatial artifact scanning, cross-modal consistency checks, and explainable AI heatmaps to highlight suspicious regions and signal potential manipulation in video content.

Spatial artifact scanning Cross-modal consistency Explainable AI heatmaps

Detection framework

Analyzes frame-level anomalies, facial blending inconsistencies, lighting shifts, and frequency-domain irregularities to build a stronger authenticity signal.

Cross-modal reasoning

Correlates visual evidence with metadata and motion clues to identify mismatches that may indicate synthetic generation or tampering.

Explainable reporting

Produces intuitive reports with confidence scores, risk summaries, and highlighted evidence so users can understand the decision instead of just receiving a label.
